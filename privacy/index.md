# NEKRO - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

NEKRO is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What NEKRO accesses: NEKRO connects only to the owner's own TikTok account, after the owner authorizes it through TikTok Login Kit. It uses that access only to read basic profile information (to confirm the right account is connected) and to upload or post videos that the owner has chosen.

What NEKRO stores: access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to talk to the TikTok API. NEKRO does not collect data from any other TikTok users.

Sharing: NEKRO does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Removing access: the owner can revoke NEKRO's access at any time in the TikTok app settings, under Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.
